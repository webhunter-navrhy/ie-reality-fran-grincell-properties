Special Features – No. 9 Wolfe Tone Street, Kilkenny

• Superb Kilkenny City location within easy walking distance of the city centre, convenient to shops, restaurants, schools, public transport and everyday amenities

• Traditional two-storey city residence, extending to approximately 617 sq. ft. (57 sq. m.) over two floors

• Attractive traditional streetscape with rendered façade, slate roof and red-brick chimney stacks with characterful dormer-style upper windows

• Two well-proportioned and versatile rooms at ground-floor level

• Front reception room featuring a traditional fireplace

• Fitted kitchen with excellent natural light from a large window

• Scope to upgrade and further develop the kitchen/dining arrangement

• Convenient ground-floor bathroom and separate WC

• Two rooms at first-floor level with characterful sloping ceilings

• Surprisingly generous enclosed rear yard/garden for such a central city location

• Excellent potential to create a private courtyard garden, outdoor dining and entertaining area

Grincell’s are delighted to market No. 9 Wolfe Tone Street. No. 9 Wolfe Tone Street is a traditional inner-city two-storey residence with direct access to a rear garden and on street parking. Set over two floors and measuring 620 sq. ft approx.. with opportunity to extend, No. 9 offers a wonderful chance to live in an established location and within 200 metres of Kilkenny train and bus depot, church, post office and shops. You even have a traditional greengrocer just 50 metres away.

Externally, the house forms part of an attractive traditional streetscape, with a simple rendered façade, slate roof, dormer-style upper windows and red-brick chimney stacks giving the property an unmistakable character.

The entrance hallway gives access to the principal ground-floor accommodation and staircase to the first floor. There are two well-proportioned rooms at ground level, measuring approximately 9'6" x 12'11" and 9'0" x 12'8". The front reception room features a traditional fireplace and offers comfortable living space, while the second room provides considerable flexibility depending on a purchaser's requirements.

The kitchen is positioned towards the rear and already incorporates a range of fitted units, worktop space and a large window providing excellent natural light. There is ample room to further develop the kitchen/dining arrangement as part of a future refurbishment.

A particularly useful feature is the ground-floor sanitary accommodation, comprising a separate bathroom and WC.

Upstairs, the first floor extends to approximately 259 sq. ft. and contains two rooms, measuring approximately 13'0" x 7'10" and 9'0" x 12'6", together with the landing. The sloping ceilings and dormer-style windows add character to this level while also creating an interesting opportunity for a purchaser to reconfigure and upgrade the space.

Outside is where No. 9 offers another pleasant surprise. To the rear lies a substantial enclosed paved yard/garden, considerably larger than might initially be expected from a property in such a central city location, it provides excellent scope to create an attractive private courtyard garden with planting, outdoor dining, seating and entertaining areas.

No. 9 Wolfe Tone Street combines a highly convenient Kilkenny City location, traditional character, two-storey accommodation and valuable private outdoor space. For purchasers prepared to undertake upgrading works, it represents a property with genuine potential to become a charming and individual city home. Ring Fran to view.
